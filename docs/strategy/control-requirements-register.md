# Atlassian Cloud: 52-Control Requirements Register

Review draft v0.2 | 21 October 2026 | Provisional profile `FS-COLLAB-01 v0.1`

**Define requirements before executable policy.** This register translates the selected 52 CCM controls into proposed requirements for Cloud Foundation, Asset Ownership and Treatment, and supporting automation. It retains the published control identifiers; it does not introduce new control domains.

## Scope, authority and interpretation

- **Pinned source:** CCM v4.1.1, `CCMv4.1.1-generated_at_2026_10_02.xlsx`, worksheet `CCM`. Each entry links to the official archive and records the specification cells ([CSA source archive](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/download/artifacts/cloud-controls-matrix-v4-1)).
- **Derived requirements:** The outcomes below are proposed customer-side interpretations, not verbatim CSA specifications or an assertion that every clause of the parent control has been satisfied. Full source wording remains authoritative for source conformance.
- **Entry granularity:** Each row groups related requirement clauses under one source control. It is not necessarily one executable rule; later decomposition must preserve the parent control reference and distinguish individual acceptance conditions.
- **Exact selection:** The 52 control entries below define this selected baseline. The remaining CCM controls, the separate SSCF capability assessment and conditional key-management additions are not silently incorporated or discarded.
- **Current status:** Every entry is **Draft; acceptance not tested; automation coverage not verified**. The working profile below is **Assumed for design and synthetic testing**, not approved firm policy. Named accountable owners remain unappointed; role names are proposed responsibilities.
- **Platform boundary:** Consume approved asset facts, classifications, supplier decisions, privacy conditions, review decisions, incident routes and retention requirements. Do not recreate discovery, procurement, privacy assessment or enterprise approval processes.
- **Existing scope:** New migration, archive, production-copy and Rovo services remain excluded. Requirements concerning them establish prerequisites and restrictions, not authority to deliver them.

The implementation scope and asset prerequisites align to the current [Cloud Foundation](01-cloud_foundation.md) and [Asset Ownership and Treatment](02-asset_ownership_treatment.md) review drafts. This register does not update or approve either document.

## Provisional firm profile

**Profile `FS-COLLAB-01 v0.1` supplies the working values for this register.** Its scope is internal Jira and Confluence collaboration, not core banking or transaction records. More critical workloads require a separately assessed profile; personal-account tests use synthetic data only.

- **Parameter references:** `P-01` to `P-22` identify profile parameters, not additional controls or a new control taxonomy. Bindings in the input column are local design links, not official CSA mappings.
- **Authority:** These assumptions are authorised for design and synthetic validation only. They do not authorise production deployment, real-data processing, actual access grants or regulatory compliance claims.
- **Precedence:** Preserve the pinned source requirements and apply stricter applicable firm obligations. A conflicting firm policy or legal requirement triggers profile revision and re-evaluation, not silent substitution.
- **Versioning:** Evidence and derived policy shall record the register revision, profile ID/version and applicable parameter IDs. Material parameter changes invalidate affected plans and acceptance results.
- **Timing:** Minutes/hours are elapsed time; days are calendar days. Quarterly, six-monthly and annual reviews mean no more than three, six and twelve calendar months between completed reviews. Daily checks mean no more than 24 hours between usable checks.
- **Clock origins:** Privilege duration starts at activation; revocation starts at the authoritative withdrawal decision; incident response starts at detection. Vulnerability deadlines start at the first applicable actionable advisory or confirmed finding. RTO runs from service interruption to restored usable service; measure RPO loss against the interruption point. Record upstream publication/notification delays separately; receipt or retry must not reset a running deadline.
- **Target versus capability:** These are required design targets, not measured SaaS guarantees. Unsupported enforcement, latency or recovery creates an explicit gap.

### Identity, access and data protection

| Parameter | Working assumption |
| :--- | :--- |
| **P-01 · Authentication** | Firm identity provider and MFA for human users; phishing-resistant MFA for administrators. Emergency accounts are separately controlled. Authentication and effective authorization require separate tests. |
| **P-02 · Privilege** | Separate administrative identities; human elevation lasts **one hour**. No standing human privilege except approved emergency access. Human grant expiry is not a service-identity lifetime or session-timeout setting. |
| **P-03 · Access review** | **Quarterly** for privileged, service-account, external and Confidential/Restricted access; **six-monthly** for standard Internal access. Apply the shortest applicable interval and review after material change. |
| **P-04 · Revocation** | Effective withdrawal within **15 minutes** for compromised accounts and urgent leavers; **four hours** for routine removals. Test account, session, token and app-mediated access separately. |
| **P-05 · Asset admission** | Every active project/space requires a valid Business Application association, accountable owner, validated classification and approved standard. Missing authority blocks admission or access expansion; existing assets follow scoped containment rather than automatic deletion. |
| **P-06 · Sharing** | Anonymous access and public links disabled. External access requires approved recipients, a named sponsor and expiry within **30 days** unless renewed. Team-scoped access remains the default. |
| **P-07 · Ingress and egress** | Approved identities, clients, integrations and destinations only. Human interactive administration and Confidential/Restricted access require managed devices; non-human paths require approved service identities and execution contexts. Assess browser, API, app, callback and export paths separately; firm controls are not assumed to mediate SaaS-to-SaaS traffic. |
| **P-08 · Placement** | **EU** provisionally selected for supported product data. Metadata, support access, apps, backups and other processing need separate assessments; product residency does not establish whole-service EU residency. |
| **P-09 · Add-ons and AI** | Installation and consent restricted to platform administrators. Each integration needs approved scopes, data access and destinations, plus asset-specific permission. Rovo activation remains outside immediate scope. |

| Parameter | Working assumption |
| :--- | :--- |

| **P-11 · Safe execution** | Observe actual state before applying changes. Bind approval to the candidate revision, target scope and relevant authoritative inputs; material changes invalidate the plan. Destruction still requires explicit authorization. |
| **P-12 · Drift** | Observe critical security settings at intervals no greater than **15 minutes**; other managed configuration **daily**. Automatic correction is restricted to explicitly authorised, tested and reversible actions. Unreadable state is unknown, not conformant. |

### Evidence and service protection

| Parameter | Working assumption |
| :--- | :--- |
| **P-16 · Operational logs** | **90 days searchable; 365 days total protected retention**, including the searchable period. Collect into firm-controlled storage where native coverage/retention is insufficient; holds override routine disposal. |
| **P-17 · Decision evidence** | Retain material approvals, exceptions, releases and control-acceptance records for **seven years after closure or supersession**. Apply separate schedules to business content, raw telemetry and other record categories. |
| **P-18 · Monitoring health** | Detect collection failure within **five minutes** and notify immediately once known. Use explicit health signals; an empty event stream is not a healthy-collector signal. Source event-delivery delay is measured separately. |
| **P-19 · Recovery** | Collaboration-service target **RTO 24 hours / RPO 24 hours**; daily protected recovery points and **quarterly restoration tests**. Distinguish provider outage, content restore and configuration recovery; these are targets, not supplier commitments or authority for a new copy service. |
| **P-20 · Incident response** | Assume an existing **24×7** security function. Critical-event triage within **15 minutes** and containment target **one hour**, using pre-authorised actions. Coverage, routes and action authority require evidence. |
| **P-21 · Vulnerabilities** | Firm-managed automation/dependencies: **daily advisory checks**, critical remediation **seven days**, high **14**, medium **30**, low **90**. Active exploitation triggers containment within **24 hours**; this does not extend an earlier applicable deadline. Provider-operated components use separate supplier treatment. |
| **P-22 · Non-production** | **Synthetic data only** initially. No production copies, real customer information or firm secrets in the personal-account lab. Use isolated lab-specific test identities/credentials; production-copy services remain disabled. |

These periods are design assumptions, not universal legal minima. In particular, business-content retention requires a category-specific schedule; UK GDPR does not prescribe one blanket retention period ([ICO storage-limitation guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-protection-principles/a-guide-to-the-data-protection-principles/storage-limitation/)).

## Common acceptance and failure rules

- **Current authority:** Required inputs must identify their authoritative system, subject/resource, scope, version, decision authority and applicable validity. Revalidate before dependent execution; a code merge cannot replace business approval.
- **Explicit parameters:** Use the bound provisional values for design and synthetic tests; do not treat them as unresolved blanks or approved firm policy. Withhold production acceptance and dependent real-data release until required authority and capability evidence exist. Parameters absent from this profile remain explicit prerequisites, not permissive defaults.
- **Actual enforcement:** A declaration or successful write is insufficient. Verify observed configuration and representative permitted/denied behaviour; retain unsupported paths as gaps.
- **Safe change:** Restrict execution to declared managed properties/resources. Recheck stale plans, coordinate overlapping changes, retry from observed state and require explicit destructive intent.
- **Evidence:** Retain control ID, requirement revision, profile ID/version and parameter bindings, target/scope, observation time, policy/pattern/tool revisions, authoritative input references, approvals, actions, results and findings. For timed controls, record the clock origin, deadline and measured completion/delay. Apply P-16/P-17 by record category; protect access/integrity and minimise sensitive content.
- **Honest posture:** Report conformant within tested scope, approved exception, nonconformant or unknown/stale. An exception is not a passed control; missing evidence must not become an allow decision.
- **Proportionate failure:** Block the affected new grant, change, flow or release. For existing workloads, invoke the approved containment/escalation rule; do not cause an indiscriminate shutdown or destroy retained information.
- **Permissible exceptions only:** Validate scope, approver, compensating controls, expiry and remediation owner. Expiry or withdrawal triggers reassessment; no exception can waive a legal obligation.
- **Responsibility:** The platform operating owner coordinates acceptance. Business/data, Security/GRC, IAM, Network, Records/Legal and supplier owners retain decisions within their mandates; inherited/provider controls require evidence, not assumed satisfaction.
- **Acceptance labels:** A synthetic test may meet a provisional target without establishing production acceptance. Record assumption status, capability coverage and test result separately; never label the profile implemented or the service compliant solely because parameter values exist.

## Readiness to translate into executable policy

For each requirement, bind its profile parameters and authoritative inputs to the specific resource/action, enforcement location, read/write/test support and evidence contract. Scope approved automatic actions separately from observation. Classify execution as supported automation, partial automation, inherited/provider assurance or controlled manual treatment; none is verified by this draft.

- **Design and lab:** Use `FS-COLLAB-01 v0.1` and synthetic fixtures to test policy decisions, timed boundaries, denied paths, stale inputs, partial failures and evidence output. This is now a defined target, not a list of wholly unresolved thresholds.
- **Production:** Confirm applicable firm authority, fill the remaining inputs and demonstrate capability before dependent real-data use. Unsupported controls retain explicit gap treatment; do not lower a target merely because the SaaS interface cannot meet it.
- **Policy contract:** Each atomic rule shall carry its parent CCM ID, register/profile revision, parameter references, subject/action/resource, required inputs, decision/failure behaviour and acceptance-test/evidence references. This defines the contract without selecting a runtime or bespoke controller.
- **Re-evaluation:** A changed parameter, authority, supplier capability or managed pattern invalidates affected results and triggers scoped retesting. Preserve prior evidence and its original profile binding.

Use SSCF capability tests to assess the available SaaS interfaces, without treating SSCF IDs as an official one-to-one mapping to these CCM entries. Do not import older bundled JSON as an exact representation of the pinned worksheet.

The register remains complete for the same selected 52-control profile, not the full CCM catalogue or the firm's entire legal obligations. Version 0.2 adds the provisional parameter profile, control bindings and corresponding acceptance conditions; it does not add source controls, approve firm policy or declare implementation. Publication does not select a policy engine, amend the main platform strategy or change the tenant.
